Where to convert

__ARTICLE__

Where your file goes

01 · cloudflare

Images

Cloudflare Images re-encodes them. You set the quality and a maximum width.

  • PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF
  • up to 20 MB per image
02 · cloudflare

Video

Stream and Media Transformations do the work, and a Workflow keeps an eye on each job.

  • MP4 (H.264), M4A audio, still frames
  • up to 200 MB and 10 minutes
03 · your browser

Everything else

ffmpeg.wasm runs inside this tab. Nothing gets uploaded, which also means your laptop does the work.

  • WebM, GIF, MP3, WAV, OGG, MP4
  • as big as your memory allows
